# Recipe: product packshot

1. Start from one clean product reference, preferably with sufficient resolution and minimal perspective distortion.
2. Use I2V when the product must begin exactly from a designed composition.
3. Add a second image only if another surface/material is invisible in the first view.
4. Keep camera movement restrained: 15-30° orbit or a slow dolly, not both at extreme values.
5. One product action: rise, rotate, open, pour, settle — not several in one shot.
6. Keep generated typography out of the final deliverable.
7. Score product fidelity at 1.5x weight; reject beautiful takes that redesign the object.
