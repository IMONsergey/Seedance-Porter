# Recipe: multi-shot story

For anything longer than one robust generation:

1. Write the complete narrative outcome.
2. Divide it into 4-8 second clip contracts.
3. Generate only Clip 01.
4. Review accepted footage and record its actual final frame/state.
5. Build Clip 02 from that observed state, not from the old screenplay assumption.
6. Keep a continuity ledger for subject, wardrobe, environment, light, prop ownership and screen direction.
7. Intercut rather than forcing three or more characters into a fragile single shot.
8. Finish timing, titles, exact music and graphics in post.
