# One-variable retake protocol

A retake is an experiment. If you change everything, you learn nothing.

## Score first

Rate 0-5:

1. identity/product fidelity
2. motion/physics
3. camera/composition
4. temporal continuity
5. audio sync
6. artifact control
7. brief match

## Retake by the weakest dimension

### Identity/product fidelity weak

- reduce competing references;
- state the identity/product role earlier;
- use a cleaner multi-view reference;
- reduce extreme rotation if unseen geometry is being invented.

### Motion/physics weak

- reduce verbs;
- give one trajectory and one endpoint;
- shorten duration;
- use a motion reference only, explicitly excluding its style/identity.

### Camera weak

- keep one primary camera move;
- state support and endpoint;
- avoid contradictory camera language.

### Continuity weak

- split the clip;
- add explicit wardrobe/environment/spatial locks;
- continue from the accepted clip's observed final state.

### Audio weak

- tie one sound to one event;
- simplify ambience;
- move exact music/vocal production to post when sync is non-negotiable.

### Artifacts weak

- reduce fine hand/object contacts;
- simplify background crowds;
- remove critical text/logo/UI from generation;
- use shorter shots.

### Brief match weak

- move objective/action to the opening line;
- remove decorative language;
- use fewer references.

## Attempt budget

For a serious commercial shot:

- Take 1: concept/proof.
- Take 2: repair the single weakest factor.
- Take 3: second surgical repair or seed variant.
- After Take 3: reconsider shot design instead of blindly burning credits.
